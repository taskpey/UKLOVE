UK Love Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

UK Love is a chat and calling app made by UMAR RAJPOOT. For any question, contact us at umardrazofficial12@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account: your username and the email address or phone number you sign up with. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication, we never see it in plain text.

Profile: display name, the "About" line and profile photo you choose.

Content: messages, photos, voice notes, polls and status updates you send, plus a call history (who, when, how long). Call audio and video are not recorded.

Activity: online / last seen, typing and "seen" status. You can hide last seen in Settings.

Device: a notification ID (through OneSignal) linked to your user ID so we can deliver notifications.

3. Permissions

Camera (photos and video calls), Microphone (voice notes and calls) and Notifications. They are used only when you use those features. You can change them anytime in your phone settings.

4. How we use information

To run the app, deliver your messages and calls, send notifications, show your contacts, handle reports and blocks, and give you support.

5. Services we use

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores accounts and chats. OneSignal and a Cloudflare Worker send notifications. STUN/TURN servers help connect calls; calls go directly between devices when possible, otherwise they may pass through a relay server. GitHub Pages hosts the app files.

6. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Messages are stored on Firebase servers and are not end-to-end encrypted, so please do not share passwords, card or bank details in chats.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. Other users can see your name, photo, About line, online status and the messages you send them. The services in section 5 process data only to run the app. We may share information if the law requires it.

8. Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account in Settings, then Delete my account, or email us. Deleting your account removes your profile and login. Messages you already sent may stay visible to other people in the chat unless you delete them first ("Delete for everyone").

9. Children

UK Love is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has joined, email us and we will remove the account.

10. Your choices

Hide last seen, block or report users, delete messages, turn notifications off, or delete your account at any time.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact
